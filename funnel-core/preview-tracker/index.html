Category report

Each appointment category is reported separately. Enter preview ad spend per category. Visits are landing-page views in this browser (attributed = arrived with UTM tags).

CategorySpendVisits (attributed)Bookings · rateVerified qualified · rateRecipient accepted · rateHeld · attendanceNo-shows / cancelsRejectedFunded · rateCost per booking / qualified / held / funded

Definitions (denominators)

  • Booking rate = bookings ÷ landing-page visits for that category.
  • Verified-qualified rate = bookings that ever reached Qualified ÷ all bookings in the category.
  • Acceptance rate = bookings that reached Accepted ÷ bookings that reached Qualified.
  • Attendance = Held ÷ (Held + No-show). Cancellations are excluded and shown separately.
  • Funded rate = funded cases ÷ Held. Funded is reported by the recipient after the appointment.
  • Cost per X = category ad spend ÷ X. Non-owners never enter category A, so they can’t inflate its qualified count.

Status definitions

  • BookedConsumer chose a time and gave the required consent.
  • QualifiedLiberty Pointe verified it meets the category’s acceptance criteria.
  • AcceptedThe recipient (Dave’s agency) accepted the appointment under the category’s acceptance criteria.
  • HeldThe appointment took place.
  • RejectedFailed verification or was declined by the recipient, with a reason code.
  • No-show CancelledRecommended extra statuses so these aren’t counted as rejected.
