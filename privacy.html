Who We Are and What We Do

Liberty Pointe Financial LLC ("Liberty Pointe Financial," "we," "our," or "us") provides educational content, marketing, and appointment-setting services for licensed financial professionals. When you request information or book an appointment through this website, you are generally booking with an independent licensed financial professional, not with a Liberty Pointe Financial adviser. The name of the professional offering each appointment is shown before you book. Financial professionals compensate us for these marketing and appointment-setting services.

1. Information We Collect

We may collect the following types of information:

  • Contact information you submit through our forms or booking pages, such as your name, email address, and phone number
  • Appointment details you provide, such as your preferred time and the topic you want to discuss
  • If you request an appointment, your answers to short qualification questions for that appointment type. For an insurance policy review, these include whether you own an indexed universal life policy, who owns it, the approximate year it was issued, an approximate annual premium range, and your state of residence. For an insurance education consultation, these include whether you already own an indexed universal life policy, what you want to learn, what life insurance you have today, your age range, and your state of residence. We do not ask for health details, account numbers, or Social Security numbers.
  • A record of the consents you give when booking, including the consent wording shown to you, the appointment type and professional it applies to, and when you agreed
  • How you found us, such as the ad campaign or link tags in the web address you arrived from, and the website that referred you
  • Information you provide during communications with us or with the professional you book with
  • Website usage data collected through cookies and similar technologies, including the Meta Pixel, as described in the Cookies and Advertising section below

We do not ask you to submit account numbers, Social Security numbers, or detailed financial records through this website, and you should not include them in form messages.

2. How We Use Your Information

We use the information we collect to:

  • Respond to your inquiries and deliver educational content you have requested
  • Schedule, confirm, and remind you about appointments
  • Share your information with the licensed financial professional you book with, as described in Information Sharing
  • Operate, measure, and improve our website, educational content, and advertising
  • Send SMS messages related to your inquiry, appointment reminders, and educational content, only with your consent (see SMS Communications)

3. SMS Communications

By submitting your phone number through our website or registration forms and agreeing to the consent text shown with the form, you consent to receive text messages from Liberty Pointe Financial and from the licensed financial professional you book with regarding your inquiry, appointment scheduling and reminders, and related educational content. Message and data rates may apply. Message frequency varies. Reply STOP at any time to opt out. Reply HELP for assistance.

We do not share your phone number with third parties for their own unrelated marketing purposes. Sharing described in this policy is limited to the professional you book with and the service providers who help us operate this service.

4. Information Sharing

We do not sell your personal information to data brokers or mailing-list companies. Because of how our service works, we share information in these specific ways:

  • With the professional you book with. When you request or book an appointment, we provide the information you submitted to the licensed financial professional (and their agency or firm, if applicable) who will conduct your appointment. That professional uses it to contact you, prepare for, and conduct your appointment, and their use of your information is governed by their own privacy practices.
  • With service providers. We use trusted providers to operate our website, calendar/booking, customer records, and email/SMS delivery — Netlify (website hosting and forms), Calendly (scheduling), Resend (email), and Twilio (text messages) — who process information only to provide those services to us.
  • With advertising platforms. As described below, website usage data is shared with Meta (Facebook/Instagram) to measure and improve our advertising.
  • For legal reasons. We may disclose information if required by law, regulation, or legal process, or to protect our rights or the safety of others.

Financial professionals compensate us for marketing and appointment-setting services. We do not share your information with other financial professionals for marketing unless you ask us to connect you with a different professional.

5. Cookies and Advertising (Meta Pixel)

Our website uses cookies and similar technologies, including the Meta Pixel, a tool from Meta Platforms, Inc. The Meta Pixel collects information about how visitors use our website — such as pages viewed and actions taken on the site — and shares it with Meta. We and Meta use this information to measure the effectiveness of our advertising, to show our ads to people who have visited our website, and to find new audiences for our educational content.

We do not send Meta your name, email address, phone number, your answers to our qualification questions, or details about any insurance policy you own. If the Meta Pixel is used on our appointment and booking pages, it is limited to the pages you visit and whether a booking was completed, and we keep qualification answers out of page addresses so they are not shared through page-visit data.

You can control cookies through your browser settings. You can adjust how Meta uses data for advertising in your Facebook/Instagram ad settings.

6. Your Rights

You have the right to request access to, correction of, or deletion of your personal information, and to opt out of marketing communications at any time. To exercise these rights, contact us at info@libertypointefinancial.com. If your information has been shared with a professional you booked with, you may also need to contact that professional directly to exercise rights over their copy of your information.

7. Data Security

We implement appropriate security measures to protect your personal information from unauthorized access, alteration, or disclosure. However, no method of transmission over the internet is 100% secure.
