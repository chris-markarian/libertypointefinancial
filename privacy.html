Privacy Policy

Last updated: October 22, 2026

Liberty Pointe Financial LLC ("we," "our," or "us") is committed to protecting your privacy. This Privacy Policy explains how we collect, use, and safeguard your personal information when you visit our website or interact with our services.

1. Information We Collect

We may collect the following types of information:

  • Name, email address, and phone number submitted through our contact forms or consultation booking pages
  • Information you provide during consultations or communications with us
  • Website usage data, such as the pages you view, the page that referred you, your IP address, and your browser and device type, collected through cookies and similar technologies, including the Meta Pixel (see Section 6)

2. How We Use Your Information

We use the information we collect to:

  • Schedule and confirm consultations and appointments
  • Send appointment reminders and follow-up communications
  • Deliver educational content and resources you have requested
  • Respond to your inquiries
  • Send SMS messages related to your inquiry, appointment reminders, and educational content (only with your consent)
  • Improve our website and services
  • Measure how well our advertising works and show relevant ads on Meta platforms such as Facebook and Instagram

3. SMS Communications

By submitting your phone number through our website or registration forms, you consent to receive text messages from Liberty Pointe Financial regarding your inquiry, appointment reminders, and educational content. Message and data rates may apply. Message frequency varies. Reply STOP at any time to opt out. Reply HELP for assistance.

We do not share your phone number with third parties for their own marketing purposes.

4. Information Sharing

We do not sell, trade, or rent the personal information you submit to us, such as your name, email address, or phone number. We may share your information with trusted service providers who assist us in operating our website and conducting our business, provided they agree to keep your information confidential.

Our website also uses the Meta Pixel, which shares website usage data with Meta Platforms, Inc. for advertising and measurement, as described in Section 6. Under some state privacy laws, this kind of sharing for advertising may be considered a "sale" or "sharing" of personal information. See Section 7 for how to opt out.

5. Data Security

We implement appropriate security measures to protect your personal information from unauthorized access, alteration, or disclosure. However, no method of transmission over the internet is 100% secure.

6. Cookies, Tracking, and the Meta Pixel

Our website uses cookies and similar technologies to operate the site, understand how visitors use it, and measure our advertising.

Meta Pixel. We use the Meta Pixel, a tool provided by Meta Platforms, Inc. (the company behind Facebook and Instagram). When you visit a page on our website, the Pixel sends Meta information about that visit, including the page address, the referring page, your IP address, your browser and device information, and identifiers stored in cookies. Meta may connect this information with a Facebook or Instagram account you have. We use the Pixel to count visits that come from our ads and to show our ads to people who may be interested in our services.

We do not send Meta the contents of the forms on our website, such as your name, email address, phone number, or financial details. Meta's use of the information it receives is governed by the Meta Privacy Policy.

Your choices. You can limit this tracking in several ways:

  • Block or delete cookies, including third-party cookies, through your browser settings, or use a browser or extension that blocks tracking.
  • Manage how Meta uses information from other websites through your Facebook or Instagram ad preferences, including the "Activity information from ad partners" setting.
  • Opt out of interest-based advertising from participating companies at optout.aboutads.info.
  • Contact us at info@libertypointefinancial.com to ask us to opt you out of the sharing described in this section.

7. Your Rights

You have the right to request access to, correction of, or deletion of your personal information. Depending on where you live, you may also have the right to opt out of the use of your personal information for targeted advertising, or of its "sale" or "sharing." To exercise these rights, contact us at info@libertypointefinancial.com. You can also use the choices described in Section 6.
